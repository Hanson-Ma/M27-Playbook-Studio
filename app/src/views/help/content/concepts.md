# Gameplan: concepts & tags

The **Gameplan** screen helps you plan a playbook like a coach: sort plays into your own concepts (Mesh, Snag, Inside
Zone, Two-minute…), then check what you have for each formation and each situation. Tags live only in the app
(`app-data/concepts.json`); the game never sees them.

Open it with the **GAMEPLAN** button at the top right of the **PLAYBOOK** tab or of the **LIBRARY**. The back link at
the top left takes you back to where you came from.

## Start your concept list

The first time, the app offers a ready-made list.

![The first visit: create the concepts file with 39 ready-made categories, or start empty.](/guide/concepts-create.png)

1. Click **CREATE CONCEPTS FILE** for 39 common categories in three groups: **PASS** (Mesh, Snag, Smash, Flood,
   Y-Cross, Dagger, Stick…), **RUN** (Inside Zone, Outside Zone, Counter, Power…) and **OTHER** (Screens, Play
   Action, RPO, Two-minute, Red Zone, Shot Play…). Or click **START EMPTY**.
2. Press **⌘S / Ctrl+S** (or **SAVE** at the top right). The file isn't written until you save.

## Tag your plays (TAG PLAYS)

![TAG PLAYS: your playbook's plays as rows, with suggested tags on the right of each row and the tag list for the selected play.](/guide/concepts-tag.png)

1. Choose which plays to list: **PLAYBOOK** (pick one in the menu next to it; it starts on the playbook you used
   last), **CUSTOM** (your plays), **SET**, **SEARCH** or **TAGGED**.
2. Click a row. The panel on the right shows the play and every category as a chip: click a chip to tag the play,
   click it again to remove the tag.
3. The **SUGGESTIONS** column proposes tags from the play's type, its read concept, its routes and its name. Click a
   suggestion to accept it, its **×** to dismiss it, or **ACCEPT ALL** for every suggestion in the list. Nothing is
   tagged until you accept it.
4. To tag many plays at once, tick their boxes, then click a category in the right-hand panel.
5. Save with **⌘S / Ctrl+S** (or **SAVE** at the top right) when you're done.

**UNTAGGED** and **HAS SUGGESTIONS** filter the list; **GROUP** sorts rows by set or category. Each play can also
have a note.

## Manage categories (CATEGORIES)

Add a category, rename it, give it a color, drag it onto another to nest it (for example "Mesh Rail" under "Mesh"),
move it between PASS, RUN and OTHER, or delete it (its tags are removed from every play).

## Check your gameplan

| Tab | Answers |
|:--|:--|
| **RUN MATRIX** | Which run concepts you have in which formation, with the gaps highlighted |
| **PASS MATRIX** | The same for pass concepts |
| **SITUATIONS** | What you have for each situation (1st down, 3rd & short, red zone, two-minute…), using the plays' CPU weights. Template sections count too |
| **COVERAGE** | How many plays each category has in the playbook |

![SITUATIONS: the plays STUDIO would call on 1st down, by CPU weight.](/guide/concepts-situations.png)

Your categories also show up as filters in the [Library](#/help/library) (FILTERS → CONCEPT) and as the CONCEPT tab
of [Preview in game](#/help/preview).
